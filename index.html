In my own words The Engineer
Behind the Code

"I build at the intersection of craft and impact."

I'm Katha — a Senior Software Engineer who genuinely loves the work. I hold an MS in Computer Science from USC and I've spent my career moving between machine learning research and production-grade web engineering, finding that the two are more connected than people think.

I trained the first end-to-end American Sign Language Production model at USC, shipped scalable marketing systems at Druva that serve millions of visitors, and built secure intranet tools for remote sensing operations at the Indian Space Research Organisation. Each role taught me something different about what it means to build things people depend on.

My work is guided by a simple standard: technically sound, ethically grounded, and never shipped until it's truly complete. I don't optimize for done — I optimize for right.
